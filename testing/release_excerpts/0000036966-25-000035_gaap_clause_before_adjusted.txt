First Horizon Corporation's Momentum Continues with Strong First Quarter 2025 Results Net Income Available to Common Shareholders of $213 Million with an EPS of $0.41, a $0.12 Increase from Prior Quarter; $217 Million or $0.42 on an Adjusted Basis, Down $0.01 from Prior Quarter* MEMPHIS, TN (April 16, 2025) – First Horizon Corporation (NYSE: FHN or “First Horizon”) today reported first quarter net income available to common shareholders ("NIAC") of $213 million or earnings per share of $0.41, compared with fourth quarter 2024 NIAC of $158 million or earnings per share of $0.29.
